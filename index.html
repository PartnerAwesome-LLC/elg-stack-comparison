2. Crossbeam has quietly become a two-time acquirer in this exact category

Beyond Reveal, Crossbeam also acquired Partnered on May 18, 2022, rebranding it as "Crossbeam Sales Edge." That means of the original 8 names in this research brief, only 6 remain independently operating companies: Crossbeam, PartnerStack, Impartner, ZINFI, Allbound/Channelscaler, and WorkSpan.

A "state of the ELG market" or "partnership tech landscape 2026" piece that calls out this consolidation trend directly fills a real gap — most existing landscape graphics and blog posts still list Reveal and Partnered as active, independent vendors.

Why this matters for partnerships: buyers evaluating "best-of-breed" ecosystem stacks should know two of their shortlist names already collapsed into one vendor — Crossbeam now owns account mapping, co-sell, and attribution in a single platform.

4. ZINFI vs. Impartner is an active, underserved rivalry

ZINFI runs its own head-to-head comparison pages against Impartner (see zinfi.com/compare), leaning on its G2 rating advantage (4.9 vs. 4.5) while remaining bootstrapped against Impartner's $113M+ in VC backing (Brighton Park Capital; The SaaS News).

5. Category-wide consolidation: PRM and account-mapping are merging into "ecosystem revenue platforms"

Crossbeam's own positioning has shifted from a pure data-escrow/account-mapping tool toward a full "Ecosystem Revenue Platform" spanning mapping, co-sell (Sales Edge), and attribution — the same trajectory seen at Impartner (adding HyperscalerGTM and Aimi AI) and ZINFI (folding co-selling into its Unified Partner Management suite).

Why this matters for partnerships: category labels like "PRM" or "account mapping" are becoming less useful for vendor selection — evaluate platforms on the full breadth of ecosystem functions they now cover, not their original category tag.
