## Executive summary

Of the 8 vendors originally scoped for this research, only **6 are still independently operating products**. Crossbeam has quietly consolidated the account-mapping and co-sell category by acquiring two of the other seven names on this list — **Partnered** (2022) and **Reveal** (2024) — and folding both into its own platform. Separately, **Allbound** merged with Channel Mechanics in 2025 to form a new combined brand, **Channelscaler**, which is why its G2 identity has shifted. This matters for anyone building comparison content, since several "vs." search terms in this space are now historical rather than live competitive comparisons.

## Notable rating / pricing gaps

1. **ZINFI has the highest G2 rating in the category (4.9/5, 722 reviews)** — the highest-rated PRM in G2's own Summer 2026 Grid Report ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/g2-summer-2026-grid-report-names-zinfi-a-leader-in-partner-relationship-management-with-4-9-star-rating-highest-among-prm-leaders-302784020.html)) — yet it has raised **zero venture funding** ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/129995-29); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/zinfi/__DGdTSt7pmCtqWVWKE6d3BpihSq_4nPCQCrwgB0VzRx4)) and publishes **no pricing anywhere**. This is a rare combination: a bootstrapped company out-rating VC-backed competitors like Impartner ($113M+ raised) on customer satisfaction.
2. **PartnerStack removed all public pricing around 2020** despite branding itself "the #1 rated ecosystem platform" on its own [pricing page](https://partnerstack.com/pricing). Its last publicly listed prices (2019) started at $800/mo; real-world spend today, per [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/partnerstack), runs a median of ~$19,600/yr with a wide $9K-$38K range — real transparency has effectively vanished from a company whose category-defining pitch is partner-program ROI.
3. **Impartner is the only PRM in this set with a genuinely public price list** — Emerge $25K/yr, Ignite $45K/yr, Pro $75K/yr, Enterprise $150K/yr, all listed on [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-l75vriw7iffmw) — even though actual negotiated deals (Vendr median ~$54,540/yr) run well under list price.
4. **Crossbeam is the only account-mapping/ecosystem vendor with fully self-serve, published pricing**, starting with a genuine free tier — a meaningful differentiator versus every PRM and co-sell competitor in this list, all of which are "contact sales" only.

## SEO-worthy insights (high search-volume angles)

### 1. "Crossbeam vs. Reveal" is now a historical query, not a live comparison
Reveal was acquired by Crossbeam in an all-stock merger announced [June 24-25, 2024](https://tech.eu/2024/06/25/crossbeam-acquires-reveal-in-all-stock-transaction/), and the standalone Reveal platform and website were fully decommissioned on [June 1, 2025](https://www.crossbeam.com/reveal-platform-sunset), with all customers migrated to Crossbeam. Anyone still searching "Crossbeam vs Reveal" is asking about a company that no longer independently exists. Content targeting this term should be reframed as "Crossbeam (formerly Reveal): what happened and what changed for customers" — there's a real content gap here since most existing comparison pages predate the 2024 merger and are now stale.

### 2. Crossbeam has quietly become a two-time acquirer in this exact category
Beyond Reveal, Crossbeam also acquired **Partnered** on [May 18, 2022](https://insider.crossbeam.com/entry/partnered-crossbeam-acquired), rebranding it as "Crossbeam Sales Edge." That means of the original 8 names in this research brief, **only 6 remain independently operating companies** (Crossbeam, PartnerStack, Impartner, ZINFI, Allbound/Channelscaler, WorkSpan). A "state of the ELG market" or "partnership tech landscape 2026" piece that calls out this consolidation trend directly would fill a real gap — most existing landscape graphics/blog posts still list Reveal and Partnered as active, independent vendors.

### 3. Every major vendor shipped a flagship AI feature within the last 12 months
This is a genuine category-wide pattern worth its own piece: Crossbeam Copilot (2024-2025), PartnerStack AI Matches + MCP integration ([Oct-Nov 2025](https://partnerstack.com/articles/series-b)), Impartner's Aimi engine ([Dec 11, 2025](https://impartner.com/request-prm-pricing/)), ZINFI's AI-native co-selling management ([May 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/g2-summer-2026-grid-report-names-zinfi-a-leader-in-partner-relationship-management-with-4-9-star-rating-highest-among-prm-leaders-302784020.html)) and ZINFI.AI ([March 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/zinfi-technologies-once-again-named-a-leader-in-g2-grid-report-for-partner-relationship-management-prm-spring-2026-302722010.html)), and WorkSpan AI / AI Teammates ([March 27, 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250327578659/en/WorkSpan-Launches-WorkSpan-AI-to-Eliminate-the-Partner-Complexity-Tax-in-Sales-Execution)). A comparison table of "AI features across partnership platforms" targets buyers actively evaluating vendors on this exact axis right now.

### 4. ZINFI vs. Impartner is an active, underserved rivalry
ZINFI runs its own head-to-head comparison pages against Impartner (e.g., [zinfi.com/compare/zinfi-vs-impartner](https://www.zinfi.com/compare/)), leaning on its G2 rating advantage (4.9 vs. 4.5) while remaining bootstrapped against Impartner's $113M+ in VC backing ([Brighton Park Capital](https://www.bpc.com/insights/impartner-raises-50m-to-accelerate-growth); [The SaaS News](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/impartner-raises-35-million-in-funding/)). A neutral, well-sourced "ZINFI vs. Impartner" comparison (pricing, funding, satisfaction scores, ICP fit) would likely outrank vendor-authored comparison pages, which are inherently biased.

### 5. Category-wide consolidation: PRM and account-mapping are merging into "ecosystem revenue platforms"
Crossbeam's own positioning has shifted from a pure data-escrow/account-mapping tool toward a full "Ecosystem Revenue Platform" spanning mapping, co-sell (Sales Edge), and attribution — the same trajectory seen at Impartner (adding HyperscalerGTM and Aimi AI) and ZINFI (folding co-selling into its Unified Partner Management suite). This signals a maturing market bifurcating into a handful of full-stack platforms (Crossbeam, Impartner, ZINFI, PartnerStack) versus shrinking, feature-narrow niche tools — a useful market-map narrative for a "who's really competing with whom in 2026" piece.
